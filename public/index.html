Kestrelgives

Kestrel's yearly charity auction. Coworkers donate things they make and do, everyone bids on them or claims them, and every dollar goes to three charities.

It runs in three parts: a submission window, when anyone can offer an item; a few days of bidding; then time to donate and upload proof.

How to participate

  1. Submit an item

    Offer something you make or do, from a cabin weekend to a dozen lemon squares. Anyone can submit while submissions are open.

  2. Bid or buy

    Bid in the silent auction, where the highest bid at the close wins, or claim instant-sale items at a set price while they last.

  3. Donate once

    When bidding closes, donate your total once on the giving platform. Your Items shows the amount.

  4. Upload proof

    Upload a screenshot of your donation within 7 days of the close. An admin reviews it.

Where donations go

Every donation is split equally among these three charities, so you donate once and all three benefit.
